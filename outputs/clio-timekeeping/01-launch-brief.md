## What is launching
Clio Timekeeping captures your billable activity and drafts time entries for you to review. You check each entry, edit it if needed and mark it Bill-ready. Setup comes first: nothing is created until it is finished.

## Open questions
- **What is captured from the computer, and how is it handled?** The help article has no privacy wording and Legal has not cleared any. Asked by Sales Engineering and a beta managing partner. Answer: Legal, with Product.
- **Can admins or billers see other users' entries?** The help article says users see only their own. The handoff says this is not settled, and a beta billing coordinator wants it. Answer: Customer success, with Product.
- **Do we say "about 15 minutes" in marketing?** The help article says "usually", the handoff release notes drop that word. Answer: Support lead, with Product.
- **Can beta numbers or quotes be used outside Clio?** Firms have not agreed, so they stay internal. Answer: Product marketing, with Legal and the beta firms.
- **How will we measure the 15% adoption target by firm size?** Revenue operations cannot yet track setup by firm size in the CRM. Answer: Revenue operations.
- **Which growth driver does this serve, and is any competitor claim possible?** `context/company.md` names neither a driver nor a public competitor source. Answer: Product marketing leadership, Sales Engineering.
