# Readiness quiz: Clio Timekeeping GA

8 questions. Pass mark: 80%, so 7 of 8 (illustrative). Goal: at least 90% of reps pass (illustrative). Due Fri Jan 29, 2027 (illustrative).

## Questions
1. What does Timekeeping do for the lawyer? (positioning)
2. Who is the best-fit first call? (positioning)
3. What does it cost, and where is it available? (positioning)
4. A lawyer says "I already use a timer." What do you say? (objection)
5. A lawyer says "I don't want software billing for me." What do you say? (objection)
6. A partner asks what the Clio Desktop App captures. What do you say? (objection)
7. Name the four limits you state on every call. (limitations)
8. A billing coordinator asks to see the lawyers' entries. What do you say? (limitations)

## Answer key
1. It captures billable activity and drafts time entries. The lawyer reviews each one and marks it Bill-ready. | 06, Pillars 1 and 2
2. Solo and small-firm lawyers who rebuild the week from sent mail. Then managing partners with flat-fee work. | 05, Best-fit customers
3. Included on all Clio Manage plans at no extra cost. US and Canada, English (illustrative, from the handoff). | 06, Pillar 3
4. Timekeeping drafts entries from your activity while you work, and you check each one. | 05, Alternatives
5. It does not bill. It drafts. The lawyer reviews and approves, and switches on Bill-ready. | 06, Must say 4
6. Outlook and OneDrive are connected, and computer activity is captured through the Clio Desktop App. On the detail, say you will find out and come back. Make no data-handling claim. | 06, Avoid saying
7. Setup comes first. You see only your own entries. Matter suggestions can be wrong. It drafts and the lawyer approves, it does not bill on its own. | 06, Must say
8. Users can only see and review their own entries. Do not promise more. | 06, Must say 2
