## FAQ
**Does it cost extra?** No. Included on all Clio Manage plans (illustrative, from the handoff).
**Does it work right away?** No. Nothing is created until setup is finished. A guided checklist in Clio Manage shows what to connect.
**Can I see my team's entries?** You can only see and review your own. Do not promise more.
**Can it pick the wrong matter?** Yes. Suggestions can be wrong, so every entry needs review.
**Does it bill for me?** No. It drafts. The lawyer reviews and approves.
**Where is it available?** US and Canada, in English (illustrative).
