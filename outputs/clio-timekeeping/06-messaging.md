# Messaging: Clio Timekeeping general availability

## One-line value proposition
Work you did but never wrote down never reaches a bill, so Clio Timekeeping captures your billable activity as you work and drafts time entries in Clio Manage for you to check and mark Bill-ready, included on every Clio Manage plan.

## Three pillars

### Pillar 1: Stop rebuilding your week
- **Message:** Your time is drafted while you work, so Friday afternoon is for checking a list, not remembering the week.
- **Supporting points:**
  - Timekeeping captures billable activity from Outlook, OneDrive, the Clio Desktop App and calls made in the Clio mobile app.
  - For work outside the web app, such as email, it suggests a matter from names, email addresses and other available information.
  - Drafted entries appear shortly after the activity.
  - Setup comes first. A guided checklist in Clio Manage shows what to connect.
- **Proof:** Clio help article, "Clio Timekeeping" (capture sources, matter suggestion, review flow). Setup checklist from the GA build note in the handoff.

### Pillar 2: You check every entry, you decide what is billed
- **Message:** Timekeeping drafts. You review and approve. Nothing is billed until you say so.
- **Supporting points:**
  - Open Activities > "My time entries" in Clio Manage, or "View entries" in the Clio Desktop App.
  - Select an entry to see the captured activity behind it.
  - Fix anything with the pencil icon, then switch on "Bill-ready" to add the entry to the matter's unpaid bills.
  - If a suggested matter is wrong, you change it before the entry goes anywhere.
- **Proof:** Clio help article (review steps, activity behind each entry). Handoff release notes and demo notes.

### Pillar 3: Time that lands where your matters and bills already are
- **Message:** Entries arrive inside Clio Manage, on your matters, ready for your next bill. On flat-fee matters, captured time shows whether the work paid off.
- **Supporting points:**
  - Bill-ready entries go straight to the matter's unpaid bills.
  - On flat-fee matters, captured time does not appear on the invoice. It shows whether the work was profitable.
  - Included on all Clio Manage plans at no extra cost, for customers in the United States and Canada, in English.
- **Proof:** Clio help article (Bill-ready). LawSites, Oct 26, 2026 (flat-fee point). Plans and regions from the handoff (illustrative).

## Avoid saying
- "AI bills for you", "automatic billing" or anything where Timekeeping decides. It drafts, you approve.
- Any claim about what the desktop captures, privacy or data handling. Legal has not cleared one.
- Any figure for time saved, hours recovered, revenue or accuracy. None exists.
- Any beta number or quote. They are internal.
- Any competitor name or "better than" comparison.
- "Timer", "tracker" or "surveillance" framing. Say captured, drafted, reviewed.

## Must say (limitations every asset repeats)
1. Nothing is created until setup is finished.
2. You can only see and review your own entries.
3. Matter suggestions can be wrong, so every entry needs review.
4. Timekeeping drafts entries. The lawyer reviews and approves. It does not bill on its own.
