## Market category
- **Options considered:** time tracking tool; AI assistant for lawyers; time capture built into legal practice management.
- **Chosen:** time capture built into legal practice management software.
- **What it makes customers assume:** it works with their matters, time entries and bills, it is part of Clio Manage and it is included in their plan. It also makes them assume a review step, which is true.
- **Risk:** "time tracking" can sound like a timer, and "AI assistant" can sound like it acts alone. Messaging must say drafted, reviewed, approved.

## What this means for messaging
- Lead with the Friday-afternoon rebuild and the hour that never reached a bill, then the way through.
- Say drafted, reviewed, approved, in that order. The lawyer decides. Timekeeping does not bill.
- Say the four plain limits where a customer needs them: setup first, you see only your own entries, matter suggestions can be wrong, the lawyer approves.
- Name no competitor, make no privacy claim and keep beta numbers and quotes out of customer-facing copy.

## Open questions
- What does the Clio Desktop App capture, and how is it handled? Legal and Product.
- Can admins or billers see other users' entries? Customer success and Product.
- Do we say "about 15 minutes", with or without "usually"? Support lead and Product.
- Can beta results or quotes be used outside Clio? Product marketing and Legal.
- Is any comparison to other time capture tools possible, and can "AI" appear in public wording? Sales Engineering and Legal.
