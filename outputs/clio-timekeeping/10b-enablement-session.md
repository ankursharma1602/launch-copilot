## Talking points
- Lead with the Friday rebuild, then the way through.
- Say drafted, reviewed, approved, in that order.
- Four limits: setup first, own entries only, matter suggestions can be wrong, it does not bill on its own.
- On flat-fee matters, captured time stays off the invoice and shows profitability.
- No competitor names, no beta numbers, no time-saved claims.
- Unsure what the desktop app captures? Say you will find out and come back.

## Role-play 1: Solo Sam, solo attorney
- **Goal:** Get Sam to turn it on for one week.
- **Sam says:** "I rebuild my week every Friday." "I use a timer already." "Will it pick the right matter?"
- **Good looks like:** Asks discovery first, then says matter suggestions can be wrong and he checks each entry.
- **Common mistake:** Saying it bills for him.

## Role-play 2: Partner Priya, managing partner
- **Goal:** Link it to flat-fee profitability and agree one matter to try.
- **Priya says:** "What does it capture on the desktop?" "Can I see my lawyers' entries?" "Is there a number for hours recovered?"
- **Good looks like:** Honest follow-up on capture. Says each lawyer sees only their own entries. Offers no number.
- **Common mistake:** Inventing an answer on capture or results.

## Role-play 3: Billing Bailey, billing coordinator
- **Goal:** Get Bailey to share the setup checklist with lawyers.
- **Bailey says:** "Will I see everyone's entries?" "Who switches on Bill-ready?" "Does this add clean-up?"
- **Good looks like:** The lawyer switches on Bill-ready, which adds the entry to the matter's unpaid bills. No admin visibility promised.
- **Common mistake:** Promising billers can see other people's entries.
