## Release note
**Title:** Clio Timekeeping is now available on every Clio Manage plan

Clio Timekeeping drafts time entries from your billable activity, so you can check them instead of rebuilding your week. You review each entry and decide what goes on a bill. It is included on all Clio Manage plans at no extra cost.

**What's new**
- A guided setup checklist in Clio Manage shows what to connect. Nothing is created until setup is finished.
- Entries are drafted from Outlook, OneDrive, the Clio Desktop App and calls made in the Clio mobile app. For work outside the web app, such as email, it suggests a matter. Suggestions can be wrong, so check each entry.
- Review under Activities > "My time entries". Select an entry to see the captured activity behind it. You only see your own entries.

**How to turn it on**
1. In Clio Manage, connect Outlook and OneDrive.
2. In the Clio Desktop App, turn on computer activity capture.
3. Follow the setup checklist in Clio Manage.
4. Open Activities > "My time entries" (or "View entries" from the Clio Desktop App). Edit with the pencil icon, then switch on "Bill-ready" to add the entry to the matter's unpaid bills.

**Availability:** All Clio Manage customers in the United States and Canada, in English.
