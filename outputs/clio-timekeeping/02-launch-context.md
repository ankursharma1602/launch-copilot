# Launch context: Clio Timekeeping general availability

## What's shipping
Clio Timekeeping captures a lawyer's billable activity and drafts time entries for the lawyer to review. The lawyer checks each entry, edits it if needed and marks it Bill-ready. At GA it has the same capabilities as the beta, plus a guided setup checklist in Clio Manage. Nothing is created until setup is finished.

## What's built
All of this comes from the handoff (epic, release notes, demo notes). It is the build, not customer opinion.

| Capability | What the customer can do | Source | Matters most to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Connect Outlook and OneDrive in Clio Manage. Capture computer activity through the Clio Desktop App. | TIME-101, release notes | Lawyers |
| Capture | Have billable activity captured as they work, including calls made in the Clio mobile app. | TIME-102, release notes | Solo and small-firm lawyers |
| Matter suggestion | Get a suggested matter for work outside the web app, such as email, based on names, email addresses and other available information. | TIME-103, release notes | Lawyers, billing admins |
| Drafted entries | See drafted time entries about 15 minutes after the activity. | Release notes | Lawyers |
| Review | Open My time entries (Activities > "My time entries", or "View entries" in the Clio Desktop App). Select an entry to see the captured activity behind it in the right-hand panel. Edit with the pencil icon. Switch on Bill-ready to add the entry to the matter's unpaid bills. | TIME-104, release notes, demo | Lawyers |
| Flat-fee view | See captured time on flat-fee matters. It does not appear on the invoice. It shows whether the work was profitable. | TIME-105, demo | Managing partners |
| Guided setup checklist (GA only) | Follow a checklist in Clio Manage that shows nothing is created until setup is finished. | GA build note | Every firm |

### Limitations
1. Nothing is created until setup is finished.
2. Users can only see and review their own entries.
3. Matter suggestions can be wrong, so every entry needs review. (Wording marked "illustrative, confirm with Product" in the handoff.)
4. Timekeeping drafts entries. The lawyer reviews and approves. It does not bill on its own.

## What closed beta customers said
A small group of invited firms used the beta. Their feedback covered solo lawyers, managing partners with flat-fee matters and billing staff. It is separate from the build above and does not prove any capability. Internal evidence, illustrative, not for outside use.

## Open questions
1. What does the Clio Desktop App capture, and how is it handled? No privacy wording exists and Legal has not cleared any. Sales Engineering and a beta partner asked. Owner: Legal, with Product.
2. Can admins or billers see other users' entries? The help article says users see only their own, and the handoff says it is not settled. Owner: Customer success, with Product.
3. Do we say "about 15 minutes"? The help article says entries "usually" appear about 15 minutes after activity. The handoff release notes drop "usually". Support asked for plain wording. Owner: Support lead, with Product.
4. Can any beta numbers or quotes be used outside Clio? Firms have not agreed. Owner: Product marketing, with Legal and the beta firms.
5. How does the matter suggestion rank its picks, and how often is it wrong? Not documented, and not answered in the demo. Owner: Engineering lead.
6. Can we compare Timekeeping to other time capture tools or use "AI" in public wording? No public source names a competitor, and Legal must check "AI" wording. Owner: Sales Engineering and Legal.
