# Sales enablement

Reps will read this in the two minutes before a call. Write for that.

## Method
1. Check the asset matrix for this tier. Tier 3 gets only a short Sales FYI (5 lines max: what it is, who cares, one line to say, where to learn more).
2. Discovery questions come before the pitch. A rep should find the pain before naming the feature.
3. Objections come from the persona's fears in `context/personas.md` and the known limitations in the input.
4. The FAQ must include every known limitation, stated plainly.
5. Competitive lines only from `context/company.md` or the input.

## Output template

```
# Sales enablement: <release name> (Tier <x>)

## One-pager
**Headline:** <customer outcome>
**The problem:** <2 lines>
**What it does:** <3 bullets>
**Why Rewind:** <2 bullets, proof-backed>
**Who to call about it:** <segment or trigger, e.g. accounts rolling out AI agents in Jira>

## Talk track (60 seconds)
<what a rep actually says, in plain spoken sentences>

## Discovery questions
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

## Objection handling
| They say | You say | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... |

## FAQ
**Q:** ...
**A:** ...
(include every known limitation)

## Competitive quick take
| Alternative | Where they stop | Where we go further |
|---|---|---|
```
