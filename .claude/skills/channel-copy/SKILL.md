# Channel copy

## Method
1. Check the asset matrix in `context/tiering.md` for the tier in `06-campaign-strategy.md`. Produce only the copy assets that tier gets. List the skipped ones at the top so a reader knows it was deliberate.
2. Write the "Must say" limitations from `05-messaging.md` into each asset first. Then fit the rest around them. Short assets lose limitations when they are added last.
3. Every asset leads with the primary persona's moment of failure or desired outcome, then the capability, then proof.
4. Reuse the core message and value pillars. Adapt length and tone to the channel, not the meaning.
5. Use the persona angle from `05-messaging.md` for the persona each channel reaches.
6. Check `learnings/lessons.md` and apply every relevant lesson.
7. Write the on-image text for each visual the tier gets (email header, in-app banner, LinkedIn image, one-pager layout), under 30 words each. On-image text is the headline and at most one short supporting line. It carries no limitations, disclaimers or plan caveats on the email header, in-app banner or LinkedIn image: those stay in the email, in-app message and post beside them. The one-pager layout may carry one small limits line. Count the wordmark "Rewind" in the word count.

## Channel specs
| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| Customer email | Subject under 50 characters, 2 subject options. Preview text under 90 characters. Body under 150 words. One CTA. |
| In-app message | Headline under 8 words. Body under 25 words. One button label under 4 words. |
| Release note | Title, 2-3 sentence summary, "What's new" bullets, "How to get started" steps, availability line. |
| Blog post outline | Working title (2 options), hook paragraph written in full, H2 outline with one line per section, CTA. |
| LinkedIn post | Under 120 words. First line works as a standalone hook. No hashtags beyond 2. No emojis. |
