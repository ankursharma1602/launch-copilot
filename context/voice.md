# Voice and style rules

## Voice
- **Calm, competent, specific.** We sound like the person who has already restored a Jira site at 2am, not like a security vendor selling fear.
- **Admin-literate.** Use the real words admins use. Name the actual objects: issues, pages, projects, workflows, automation rules.
- **Plain.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. No jargon we can't explain in one line.
- **Confident, not loud.** State what the product does. Let proof do the persuading.

## Hard rules
- No em dashes. Use commas, colons or a new sentence.
- No exclamation marks in customer-facing copy.
- No invented numbers, customers, quotes or dates. Use `[NEED PROOF: ...]`.
- Never imply Atlassian or any SaaS vendor is unreliable. The point is shared responsibility, not blame.
- Never frame AI or automation owners as the problem. AI is the reason the safety net matters.
- Headlines say what the customer gets, not what the feature is called.

## Banned words and phrases
peace of mind, game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, best-in-class, unlock, supercharge, leverage (as a verb), robust, effortless, world-class, next-generation, synergy, disrupt

## Preferred patterns
- Problem, then capability, then proof. In that order.
- "Undo the one change, not the whole site."
- Lead with the moment of failure the reader recognizes, then show the way out.
