## What Rewind is
Rewind provides backup, recovery, business continuity, and data governance and retention for SaaS data. It started in the Shopify ecosystem and expanded to Jira, Confluence and GitHub. It protects 16 cloud applications for more than 25,000 organizations, including companies in financial services, healthcare and manufacturing. Headquarters: Ottawa, Canada.

## Where growth is coming from (per CEO Mike Potter, Aug 2026)
1. The Atlassian cloud migration
2. Shopify commerce
3. AI usage: AI agents now act directly on SaaS data, which creates new rollback and recovery needs

## Core idea
SaaS vendors protect their own infrastructure. They do not protect you from your own mistakes, your integrations, your automations or your AI agents. That data is your responsibility, and Rewind is how you protect it.

## Positioning against Atlassian's native Backup and Restore
Rewind sits on top of Atlassian's native tool. It does not replace it.
- Atlassian's native tool is built for full-site recovery. Rewind restores a single ticket, page, file or configuration without touching anything else.
- Rewind supports recovery points under 24 hours and retention beyond 30 days.
- Rewind can restore to a different instance or account, for failover, staging and emergency fallback.
- Rewind's backups live outside the vendor's platform, so they stay usable if that platform is compromised.
- Rewind covers sites above Atlassian's size limits and targets errors introduced by automations and AI-assisted workflows.

- Direct sales for larger accounts
