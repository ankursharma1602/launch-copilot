# Personas

> Demo personas for a timekeeping launch, inferred from the public Clio Timekeeping help article and ClioCon 2026 coverage. Validate against real customer research before relying on them. Nothing here is a statistic about lawyers.

## 1. The solo or small-firm lawyer (primary user)
- **Who:** Lawyer in a solo practice or a small firm. Does the legal work, the billing and often the admin.
- **Owns:** their own matters, their own time, getting paid.
- **Fear:** work they did never reaches a bill because nobody wrote it down.
- **Wants:** time entries drafted for them that they can check in a minute, matched to the right matter.
- **Language:** "matter," "time entry," "bill-ready," "unpaid bills," "billable," "write-off."
- **Turned off by:** anything that adds a step, anything that feels like surveillance, vague "AI will do your billing" claims.
- **Source basis:** the review flow in the help article (My time entries, edit, Bill-ready toggle).

## 2. The managing partner (economic buyer)
- **Who:** Partner who runs a small or mid-size firm and signs off on tools.
- **Owns:** profitability, utilization, realization, collection, firm risk.
- **Fear:** lost billable time they cannot see, and an AI tool that bills something wrong under the firm's name.
- **Wants:** every hour captured and reviewed by the lawyer, and a way to see whether work was profitable, including flat-fee work.
- **Language:** "utilization," "realization," "collection rate," "flat fee," "hybrid billing," "profitability."
- **Turned off by:** feature lists without a business reason, unclear data handling, unclear pricing.
- **Source basis:** Benchmarking and Insights metrics, flat-fee profitability and hybrid billing in the LawSites wrap-up.

## 3. The billing or office admin (influencer, reviewer of the bill run)
- **Who:** Billing coordinator, office manager or firm administrator who prepares bills and keeps the practice management system tidy.
- **Owns:** getting bills out on time, accurate matters, clean time entries.
- **Fear:** entries on the wrong matter, bills held up waiting for lawyers to finish their time.
- **Wants:** entries already on the right matter, and a clear sign of which are bill-ready.
- **Language:** "bill run," "unpaid bills," "matter," "write-down," "bill-ready."
- **Turned off by:** extra clean-up work, unclear who can see and change what.
- **Important limit:** the help article says users can only see and review their own entries. Do not promise admin visibility into other people's entries. `[NEED PROOF: what admins or billers can see]`.
- **Source basis:** inferred from the help article's billing steps. No source in this demo profiles billing admins directly.
