# Personas

> Demo personas, inferred from Rewind's public positioning. Validate against real customer research before relying on them.

## 1. The Atlassian admin (primary user)
- **Who:** Jira and Confluence administrator at a mid-size or enterprise company. Often a team of one or two.
- **Owns:** uptime, configuration, permissions, migrations, automations, and increasingly the AI tools wired into Jira.
- **Fear:** something breaks at scale and they can't undo it without restoring the whole site. They are the person everyone looks at.
- **Wants:** fast, surgical restores. Proof they can recover before the bad day happens.
- **Language:** "roll back," "restore," "sandbox," "automation rule," "bulk change," "migration," "RPO."
- **Turned off by:** fear-mongering, vague "peace of mind" copy, anything that sounds like it wasn't written by someone who has run Jira.

## 2. The IT or security leader (economic buyer)
- **Who:** Head of IT, IT Director, CISO or VP Engineering.
- **Owns:** risk, compliance, audit readiness, vendor spend.
- **Fear:** a data loss event that becomes an audit finding, a customer incident or a board conversation.
- **Wants:** clear accountability under the shared responsibility model, retention that satisfies policy, evidence for auditors.
- **Language:** "shared responsibility," "business continuity," "retention policy," "RTO and RPO," "governance."
- **Turned off by:** feature lists without risk framing, unclear pricing, anything that adds work for their team.

## 3. The AI or automation owner (emerging influencer)
- **Who:** platform or productivity lead rolling out AI agents and automation across Atlassian tools.
- **Owns:** getting AI into workflows without breaking them.
- **Fear:** an AI agent or automation rule makes thousands of wrong changes before anyone notices.
- **Wants:** a safety net so they can move fast and get approval from IT and security to deploy.
- **Language:** "agents," "guardrails," "blast radius," "undo," "human in the loop."
- **Turned off by:** being framed as the risk. Position Rewind as what lets them say yes to AI.
