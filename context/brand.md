## Colours
Rewind's primary brand colour is a warm yellow, as in the Boomerang logo. Dark navy is the secondary. Use these as the Rewind palette until confirmed.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Boomerang Yellow | `#FFC72C` (UNCONFIRMED) | The accent in every visual: highlight bars, the lifted blocks, buttons, key shapes. Never body text on a light background. |
| Secondary | Blue Zodiac (dark navy) | `#16225C` | Headlines, wordmark on light backgrounds, dark panels |
| Neutral (light) | Link Water | `#F5F8FD` | Page and canvas backgrounds, light cards |
| Neutral (dark) | Ebony | `#0E102B` | Body text, dark backgrounds |
| Neutral | White | `#FFFFFF` | Wordmark on dark backgrounds, card fill |

Rules:
- Yellow is the accent in every visual. Navy carries the type.
- The yellow hex is a placeholder for "a warm yellow". Rewind's exact value was not available: `[NEED PROOF: confirm Boomerang yellow hex with Rewind's brand team]`. The navy and neutrals come from a third-party listing and are also unconfirmed.
- No colour outside this table, except tints of navy or yellow for schematic shapes (for example 20% and 60% opacity).
- Contrast: yellow on white or Link Water fails for text. Put text in navy or Ebony. Text on a yellow fill is navy or Ebony. Navy on Link Water and white on navy both pass for headline sizes.

## Typeface
- **Cabin** (free, Google Fonts). Weights 400, 500, 600, 700. Unconfirmed, see above.
- Headlines: Cabin 700. Subheads: Cabin 600. Body and small text: Cabin 400 or 500.
- Fallback stack if the font cannot load: `Cabin, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- For SVG that is converted to PNG, embed Cabin so the render matches (for example, an `@font-face` or a `<style>` import from Google Fonts in the SVG, or a locally installed copy). Check the PNG shows Cabin, not the fallback.

## Wordmark
- Rewind's logo file is not downloaded or embedded. Never draw or trace the logo or the Boomerang mark.
- Where a visual needs the brand, set the word **Rewind** as live text in Cabin 700, in sentence case (capital R, rest lower case). No outline, shadow, gradient or effect.
- Colour: navy (Blue Zodiac `#16225C`) on light backgrounds. White `#FFFFFF` on navy or Ebony. These mirror Rewind's approved logo colourways (full colour dark, full colour white). Do not set the wordmark in yellow text on a light background.
- Placement: top left, with the safe margin around it. Clear space of at least the height of the letter "R" on every side.
- Size: the cap height must stay legible. Rewind's guidance sets a floor of 32 px for the full logo. Keep the wordmark text at least 32 px tall at the final pixel size of the visual.
- It is a stand-in set in type, not the logo. The brief must say so: "Wordmark set in type, not the official logo file."

## Visual style
- Calm, trustworthy, enterprise. Rewind's site reads serious and reliable: structured sections, integration grids, a comparison table, compliance badges, plain statements of what the product does. It sells recoverability, not alarm.
- Light, clean layouts. Link Water or white background, yellow as the accent, navy for headlines, Ebony for body text. Generous white space.
- Structure over decoration: grids, tables, cards and simple schematics. One idea per image.
- Typography carries the design. Headline clearly largest, Cabin throughout.
- No stock photos of people at screens, no sci-fi AI imagery, no red alarms or warning symbols (see `context/voice.md`).
- Do not copy imagery from rewind.com. Do not use Atlassian, Jira or other vendors' logos.
