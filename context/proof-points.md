## Clio Timekeeping
Source: https://help.clio.com/hc/articles/50852535786139-Clio-Timekeeping
- AI-powered feature that automatically captures billable activity and drafts time entries for the lawyer to review.
- It must be set up before it creates entries.
- Setup connects Outlook and OneDrive in Clio Manage, and captures computer activity through the Clio Desktop App.
- Calls made in the Clio mobile app can be logged automatically.
- For work outside the web app, such as email, it suggests a matter based on names, email addresses and other available information.
- Entries usually appear about 15 minutes after the activity.
- Review in Clio Manage under Activities > "My time entries", or "View entries" from the Clio Desktop App. Edit with the pencil icon, then switch on the "Bill-ready" toggle to add the entry to the matter's unpaid bills.
- Selecting an entry shows the captured activity behind it.
- Users can only see and review their own entries.

Source: https://www.lawnext.com/2026/10/cliocon-2026-a-quick-wrap-up-of-everything-clio-announced-today-and-its-a-lot.html (Bob Ambrogi, LawSites, Oct 26, 2026)
- Clio Timekeeping is an opt-in beta, announced at ClioCon 2026.
- It tracks activity as lawyers work, in Clio and in other applications, and prepares matter-matched time entries for review.
- For flat-fee work, captured time does not appear on the invoice, but it shows whether the work was profitable.
