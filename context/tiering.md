# Launch tiering rubric (the GTM playbook)

Score every release on the 4Cs, 1 (low) to 5 (high). Explain each score in one line, citing the input or context. Never invent commercial numbers: a missing input becomes `[NEED PROOF: ...]` and the score says it is a judgement.

| Criterion | What it measures | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | How much revenue, pipeline, expansion or retention it can move | 25% |
| Customer | How many customers it affects, and how much it matters to them | 25% |
| Competitive | Whether it closes a gap or opens an edge against the alternatives | 25% |
| Category | Whether it shapes how the market sees the category, or the company's place in it | 25% |

**Weighted score** = sum of (score x weight). Range 1.0 to 5.0.

## Tiers
| Tier | Weighted score | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 4.0 and above | Full launch: full campaign strategy, all channel copy, all visuals, full sales enablement with rep session and quiz, external announcement, full checklist |
| Tier 2 | 2.8 up to, but not including, 4.0 | Targeted launch: campaign strategy, email, in-app, release note, LinkedIn post, four visuals, sales kit with rep session and quiz, checklist |
| Tier 3 | Below 2.8 | Quiet release: short campaign strategy, in-app message and banner, release note, a short Sales FYI, checklist |

## Overrides
The Tiering agent may move a release up or down one tier for a stated reason, such as a competitive event, a regulatory deadline or a dependency on another launch. Any override must be written in the campaign strategy and the run report.
