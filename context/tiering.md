# Launch tiering rubric

Score every release on five criteria, 1 (low) to 5 (high). Explain each score in one line, citing the input or context.

| Criterion | What it measures | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Customer reach | How much of the base can use it on day one | 25% |
| Commercial impact | Pipeline, expansion or retention it can move | 25% |
| Strategic fit | How directly it serves the company's stated growth drivers in `company.md` | 20% |
| Competitive weight | Whether it opens or closes a gap against the alternatives | 15% |
| Story value | Whether it gives the market a reason to pay attention now | 15% |

**Weighted score** = sum of (score x weight). Range 1.0 to 5.0.

## Tiers
| Tier | Weighted score | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 4.0 and above | Full launch: brief, all channel copy, full sales enablement, external announcement, full checklist |
| Tier 2 | 2.8 up to, but not including, 4.0 | Targeted launch: brief, email, in-app, release note, LinkedIn post, sales one-pager and FAQ, checklist |
| Tier 3 | Below 2.8 | Quiet release: short brief, in-app message, release note, a short Sales FYI, checklist |

## Overrides
The Strategy agent may move a release up or down one tier for a stated reason, such as a competitive event, a regulatory deadline or a dependency on another launch. Any override must be written in the brief and the run report.
